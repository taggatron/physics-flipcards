GCSE Physics Flipcards — Offline Website
========================================

How to open
-----------
1. Extract (unzip) the entire Physics_Flipcards_Website folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html so the drawings appear.

There is no installation, account or internet connection required.

Using the flashcards
--------------------
- One at a time: click a card to reveal the answer, then use Next/Previous.
- Show all 16: display the full collection as a responsive grid.
- Reveal all answers: turn every card over at the same time.
- Shuffle: randomise the order and start a fresh session.
- Equations sheet: open the OCR GCSE Physics equations sheet (formatted view
  or original sheet scan) at any point during revision.
- Start over: reset the cards and your progress.
- Keyboard: press Enter or Space on a focused card to flip it; in single-card
  mode, use the left and right arrow keys to navigate.

The questions are adapted for revision from OCR GCSE Combined Science A,
J250/11 Physics (Higher Tier), June 2024. The illustrations were supplied
by the teacher.

This package is independent of the privately hosted website and can be
stored locally or uploaded to a college learning platform.
